## Why a custom license?

The intended permission is limited to **noncommercial educational use**, including modification of both code and animations. [Creative Commons explains](https://creativecommons.org/faq/) that its NonCommercial licenses are not limited to educational use and recommends against applying CC licenses to software. A custom license therefore states the requested scope directly for both media and code. The full `LICENSE.md`, rather than this summary, controls permitted reuse.

All other uses require prior written permission from **Ogetay Kayali**. Use the [repository issue tracker](https://github.com/OgetayKayali/ScientificVisualizations/issues/new) or a contact method listed on [Ogetay Kayali's GitHub profile](https://github.com/OgetayKayali) to inquire.
