# Scientific Notes: Sonic Boom and Cherenkov Radiation

The two-dimensional wavefront envelope opens behind the moving source and becomes a three-dimensional surface with the same orientation. The muon stays at the leading apex; the broad cone section trails behind. The camera turns gently from about 21.2 to 26 s. There are no spiraling points or moving markers on the surface.

The illustrative ratio of wave speed to source speed is u/v = 5/6, giving a rearward wavefront-envelope half-angle arcsin(u/v) ≈ 56.44°. This is the wavefront envelope, not a separate diagram of photon emission directions. The usual Cherenkov photon angle is complementary to this envelope angle in this construction. Sizes and time scales are illustrative.
