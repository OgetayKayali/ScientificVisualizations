# Scientific Notes: IceCube Construction and Discovery History

The timeline uses these milestones: 1987 early AMANDA work, 1990 Greenland tests, 1999 NSF proposal, construction around 2004, first string in 2005, completion in 2010, and the 2013 evidence for astrophysical neutrinos. It then returns to the 2011 and 2012 events nicknamed Bert and Ernie. Timeline stops are equally spaced visually, not proportional to elapsed years.

Maps, drilling, detector geometry, and events are symbolic. The two first-string examples use 35 and 50 sensors as representative selections, not exact reconstructions of historical data. The 28 dots represent the 2013 sample, not 28 individually certain astrophysical identifications. Bert and Ernie are schematic cascades, not archival event displays. Colors do not encode measured energy or arrival time. The final two origin possibilities do not imply equal probabilities or a measured source direction.

Sources describe the start of construction as December 2003 or the 2004 season depending on scope; this scene follows “around 2004.” Greenland tests took place in August 1990; publication followed in 1991.
