# Scientific Notes: The Standard Model and Cosmic Neutrinos

The conventional 17-tile Standard Model layout includes six quarks, six leptons, g, γ, Z⁰, W±, and H. These tiles do not count every quantum state: gluon color states share one tile, W charge states are grouped, and antiparticles are not listed separately. Neutrino subscripts identify lepton flavors. Mass, charge, and spin property tables are omitted.

The photon tile moves into its own table position. Later, three distinct neutrino tiles move to the foreground; no photon-to-neutrino transformation is implied. They are drawn after all background tiles and divider lines, preventing occlusion. Highlights do not represent actual rotation, decay, or mass ratios.

Only a neutrino serves as the messenger in the cosmic finale; the scene does not suggest that every particle in the table reaches Earth freely. The source is generic, not a reconstruction of a particular astronomical object. Travel distances, sizes, and times are compressed. The outgoing muon travels nearly along the neutrino direction, and nearby DOMs detect its light rather than the neutrino directly. This is not a measured IceCube event.

References: [CERN Standard Model](https://home.web.cern.ch/science/physics/standard-model/), [CERN Higgs boson](https://home.cern/science/physics/higgs-boson/).
