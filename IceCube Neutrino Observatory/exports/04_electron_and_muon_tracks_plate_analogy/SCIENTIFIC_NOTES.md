# Scientific Notes: Electron and Muon Tracks: A Metal-Plate Analogy

The electron and muon arise from two separate neutrino interactions; the scene does not imply that every neutrino event produces both. Most incoming neutrinos pass through without interaction. The muon/electron mass ratio is approximately 207, but neither the drawn diameter nor the number of particles represents that ratio.

The short branching electron track represents an electromagnetic shower; the longer muon track represents greater penetration at high energy. Muons also lose energy and interact. Actual range depends on energy and material, not mass alone. Particle sizes, speeds, times, and atom drawings are schematic; oscillations and some interaction products are omitted.

At 36 s, the second part begins and atoms transform into plates. The projectile comparison is a qualitative teaching analogy, not a ballistic simulation or a prediction of armor penetration. Plate properties, holes, deflections, and fragments are illustrative. No firearm operation is demonstrated. Electron and muon symbols remain visible to identify the rows independently of color.

References: [IceCube neutrino detection](https://masterclass.icecube.wisc.edu/en/icecube-and-neutrinos/detecting-neutrinos/), [tracks and cascades](https://icecube.wisc.edu/news/research/2019/11/what-can-cascade-events-tell-us-about-neutrino-sources/), [NIST muon/electron mass ratio](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Results?search_for=muon), [PDG particle passage through matter](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/experimental_methods_and_colliders.html).
